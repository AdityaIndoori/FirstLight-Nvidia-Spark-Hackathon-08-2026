Noto Sans (Regular), SIL Open Font License 1.1
https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans

Signed-distance-field glyph ranges in MapLibre's `glyphs.proto` format, used to
label roads on the offline basemap. Fetched once from
https://demotiles.maplibre.org/font/Noto%20Sans%20Regular/{range}.pbf and committed
so the box needs no glyph server and no network at run time.

Ranges present:
  0-255       Basic Latin + Latin-1 Supplement - every road name in the AOI
  256-511     Latin Extended-A - accented names
  8192-8447   General Punctuation - the en dash and directional quotes

The OFL permits redistribution, including bundled with software, provided the
licence travels with the font. Adding a range means fetching it on a networked
machine and committing the .pbf; nothing here is generated on the box.
